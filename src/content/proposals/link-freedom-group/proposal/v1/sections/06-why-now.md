On the day a TLD is released, nobody notices. Sales accumulate slowly from
there, and the new extension spends that whole time competing against every
other alternative on the shelf. Name options surface only for the people who
have already chosen to go shopping for a name. [^f458b5]

That is the shape of the traditional path: accreditation, registrar onboarding,
marketplace listing, promotional slots. It is **demand-pull**, it works, and LFG
should run it. Its ceiling is the number of people who show up shopping.

The platform channel is the opposite shape. It is **demand-capture**: it meets a
person at the moment they have just made something and need to name it. That
moment already exists inside Vercel, Replit, Webflow, Figma, Lovable and every
agentic app builder shipped in the last eighteen months. Today it ends with a
hand-off to a third-party registrar. It could end with a registration.

There is also a live TLD to do it with. **LFG already administers `.link`**,
so the channel does not have to wait for a single 2026-round application to
clear evaluation. A platform integration can ship against a delegated,
resolving TLD this quarter, bill real registrations, and produce real attach
data — which is the evidence every later conversation needs and which no
competitor in this round can currently show.

The reason to start now, rather than at delegation, is that platform integration
is a **product** decision on their side, not a marketing one. It moves on their
roadmap cycle — a quarter or two of lead time, minimum. Signed before delegation,
the channel is live on day one. Started after, the first year is spent waiting
for someone else's sprint planning.

[^f458b5]: 2026. [LFG · Link Freedom Group — the namespace](https://lfg.link). Published: 2026-09-24
