| **4 — Design & site builders** | Figma, Webflow, Framer, Squarespace, Wix, [Durable](https://durable.co/), [Webstudio](https://webstudio.is/), [Coframe](https://www.coframe.com/), [Relume](https://www.relume.io/), [TeleportHQ](https://teleporthq.io/), [GrapesJS](https://grapesjs.com/) | Largest volume, and domains are already a real revenue line — which means an existing registrar relationship to displace or sit beside | Formal BD; longest cycle, biggest prize |

maintained lists, not a one-time scrape. That census is a standing asset for
this engagement: the target list stays current without a research project.

### Why tier 3 is underrated

Everyone pitching a namespace will start at Replit and Lovable, because those
are the names in the press. Tier 3 is where the volume actually sits and where
nobody is competing for the partnership slot. Bubble alone hosts millions of
user-built applications, and platforms like Glide, Knack, Ragic and SeaTable
turn spreadsheets into deployed apps at a scale that never shows up in
developer-tool coverage. Each of those apps is a thing that needs a URL, and
each of those platforms currently ends that flow with a hand-off.

> [!note] Why Cloudflare is not tier 1
> They are the only name here that could run a namespace themselves, and they
> sell registrations at cost as a loyalty play. Approached cold and early, the
> likely outcome is a polite no plus a competitor who now knows the plan.
> Approached with two live integrations and demonstrated attach, the
> conversation is different.

> [!warning] TK — existing relationships
> Any warm paths on your side, and anywhere you are already talking, so I do not
> walk into a conversation that is already running. Same for registrar
> relationships already in motion — the platform pitch has to be consistent with
> whatever the registrar channel has already promised.
