Engage **me — Michael Staton, using the brand
[The Lossless Group](https://www.lossless.group)** — to build LFG's Value Added
Reseller channel among developer and creation platforms, and to close the first
cohort of it.

**The Lossless Group is an umbrella brand for a loose group of collaborators,
run by me.** It serves as the DBA and as a destination resource for consulting
clients. In practice that means me, plus collaborators brought in as the work
calls for them — talent on demand, and friends between things. **That cost sits
with me, not with you.** There is no bench to justify, no staffing plan to
approve, and no change order when a phase needs a skill I do not personally
have.

Concretely, the engagement produces four things:

1. **A channel thesis and one-pager per target archetype** — the version of the
   LFG pitch that a Vercel or a Bubble partnerships lead can take to their own
   product council without translation.
2. **A tiered, named target list** with routes in, warm where warm exists,
   maintained against the toolkit census rather than assembled once.
3. **A reference VAR deal structure** — commercial terms, margin treatment, and
   the technical integration shape — so each conversation starts from a
   proposal rather than a discovery call.
4. **A viable plan for mass-scale industry coverage, to meaningfully pop early
   sales** — a sequenced, costed route to the channel being live across the
   category rather than live in three places. Coverage is the objective the
   plan is built against; the plan itself is what the first month delivers.

### Go big or go home: massive coverage at launch

**A few pilots will not do it.** What year one needs is massive acceptance —
enough platforms live at once that demand does not have to be created, it gets
captured wherever someone happens to be building. At that density the channel
starts working on the partners too: once a handful of the obvious names have
shipped it, the rest are answering why they have not, and **FOMO does the
selling.** Anything less and year one is spent demonstrating rather than
selling.

### What that means you are buying

**I bring navigation wisdom, a massive network and deeply trusting
relationships, and state-of-the-art awareness across a broad range of potential
channel partners.** Those are the parts of this that decide whether it works:
the thesis, the target list, the terms, and the conversations that turn those
into signatures. **I am an elite commando at creating opportunities, and I bring
that from day one.**
